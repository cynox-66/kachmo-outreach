# Kachmo Cold Outreach Skill Blueprint

This skill is permanently integrated into Antigravity at:
1. Workspace Skill: `.agents/skills/kachmo-cold-outreach/SKILL.md`
2. Workspace Rule: `.agents/rules/kachmo-cold-outreach.md`
3. Global Skill: `~/.gemini/config/skills/kachmo-cold-outreach/SKILL.md`

Whenever you ask Antigravity to draft, revise, or review an outreach email for a prospect, it will automatically follow this exact specification.

---

### Core Parameters Active:
- **Zero Em Dashes:** Never uses `—` or `--`.
- **Zero AI Buzzwords:** Banned words like *delve, elevate, robust, seamless, synergy, testament, cutting-edge*.
- **Zero Corporate Openers:** Never opens with *"I hope this email finds you well"*, *"We were admiring your work"*, or *"I'm reaching out because"*.
- **Body Length:** Strictly 60 to 100 words.
- **Tone:** Hyper-friendly, conversational, witty, and undeniably human.
- **Fixed Signature:**
  ```
  Dev & Aadi | Kachmo Studios
  studios@kachmo.in | https://www.kachmo.in/
  ```
