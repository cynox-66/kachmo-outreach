## 3. The 5 Outreach Archetypes & The Exact Angles

### Archetype 1: Boutique Brand & Design Agencies (30 Targets, #001–#030)
* **Locations:** London, New York, San Francisco, Amsterdam, Berlin, Toronto, Dubai.
* **Who They Are:** Visual branding, graphic design, and packaging studios.
* **The Critical Rule:** **NEVER tell them their website is bad.** Their website looks great because they design for a living!
* **The Real Angle:** **Invisible Dev Overflow.** They design world-class brand identities for *their clients*, but when their clients ask for custom React/GSAP web builds, they don't have an in-house engineering team. We act as their secret, white-labeled execution squad under NDA and mutual non-solicitation.

### Archetype 2: Funded Tech Startups (25 Targets, #031–#055)
* **Locations:** US (SF, NYC), UK (London), Sweden, India.
* **Who They Are:** Seed and YC-backed product companies with real traction and engineering depth.
* **The Real Angle:** Their landing page is usually a dense feature dump, a generic dark-mode template, or has subtle mobile layout clutters. We offer a rapid 7–10 day hero and first-screen pass that converts visitors into demos in 5 seconds without code bloat.

### Archetype 3: Interactive Systems & Creative Storytelling (15 Targets, #056–#070)
* **Locations:** Norway, UK, US, Australia.
* **Who They Are:** World-renowned specialty coffee roasters (Tim Wendelboe, James Hoffmann's Square Mile), AD100 architects (Holloway Li, Bestor), luxury ceramicists (East Fork, Heath).
* **The Real Angle:** Their real-world product or space is breathtaking, but their online store is a flat, static photo grid. We pitch scroll narratives and interactive sensory journeys that match their craft.

### Archetype 4: High-Ticket Conversion & Booking Overhauls (15 Targets, #071–#085)
* **Locations:** London (Harley Street), Delhi (Panchsheel Park), Bangalore (Indiranagar), US, Australia.
* **Who They Are:** High-ticket private cosmetic doctors, dentists, estate landscape architects, boutique M&A lawyers.
* **The Real Angle:** High average transaction values (£200–£10k+), but broken Google Maps embeds, slow Wix/WordPress sites, or services locked inside PDF flip-books. We offer a clean, high-speed booking experience.

### Archetype 5: Zero-Presence Greenfield (15 Targets, #086–#100)
* **Locations:** UK, Northern Ireland, Scotland.
* **Who They Are:** Active trading contractors, salons, and practices registered at Companies House with active demand but **zero functioning website** (or parked domains).
* **The Real Angle:** A rapid 10-day single-page flagship build that turns directory visitors into direct phone calls.
